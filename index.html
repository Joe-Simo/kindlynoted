Kindly Noted

Remember what they mentioned.

Kindly Noted is a gift planner that starts with people. Each person has a page for their dates, the ideas you noted, and what you gave in past years.

  1. People first.
    Whoever's day is next sits at the top, with a ring around their initials that closes as the date gets near.
  2. Ideas, kept.
    Note an idea the moment you hear it. You will see it again when their birthday comes around.
  3. Gentle memory.
    If a new idea looks like something you already gave, Kindly Noted tells you.
  4. Calm seasons.
    Plan a holiday list with a budget and see what is left to spend.
No account, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on your iPhone. Free to use, with an optional one-time Pro purchase for more lists, per-person budgets, widgets, and reminder choices.

Coming to the App Store for iPhone, iOS 17 or later.
