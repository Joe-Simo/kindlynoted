Kindly Noted

Privacy Policy

Kindly Noted does not collect any data. Your people, gifts, and lists stay on your iPhone.

Effective October 26, 2026.

What Kindly Noted stores

The people you add (names, relationships, birthdays, other yearly dates, and notes), your gift ideas (titles, prices, notes, links, and status), your gift lists and budgets, and your reminder settings are stored only on your device, in an App Group shared with the app's widgets. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What Kindly Noted does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera. People are only the ones you type in yourself.

Backup and CSV export

If you back up or export, Kindly Noted creates a file on your device and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice. If you restore a backup, Kindly Noted reads the file you pick on your device and adds what it contains.

Purchases

Kindly Noted Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple on your device so it can unlock Pro features. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Widgets and reminders

These features run on your device using Apple's system frameworks. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Kindly Noted does not send their data anywhere.

Backups and deleting your data

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your device backup may include Kindly Noted's data, under Apple's terms. To delete everything Kindly Noted stores, delete the app from your iPhone.

Children

Kindly Noted does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
