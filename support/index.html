Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. Use Settings, Back Up to save a file you can keep anywhere, and Restore to bring it back.

I bought Pro on another device

Open Settings or the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchase. Pro is a single purchase, not a subscription, and works with Family Sharing.

Reminders are not arriving

Check that notifications are allowed for Kindly Noted in the iPhone Settings app, then set your reminder hour in the app's Settings.

Birthdays on February 29

In years without February 29, the reminder and countdown use February 28.

Deleting everything

Delete the app from your iPhone. That removes all of its data.
